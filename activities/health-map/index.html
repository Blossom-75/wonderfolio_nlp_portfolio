ACTIVITY 06

Health Map

A location-based, disease-aware healthcare recommendation system that identifies and ranks nearby healthcare facilities based on disease-specific treatment capabilities and travel accessibility.

NER · Information Extraction · Semantic Similarity · Text Classification

Research Gaps

01

Disease-specific facility matching

Identifies healthcare centres that provide services relevant to the particular disease.

02

Treatment availability

Considers verified diagnostic and treatment capabilities of the healthcare centre.

03

Travel accessibility

Ranks suitable facilities based on road distance and estimated travel time.

Role of NLP

01

Healthcare information extraction

Extract healthcare services and treatment capabilities from facility descriptions and other textual sources.

02

Understanding natural-language queries

Identify disease names, medical services and user search intent from natural-language queries.

03

Semantic matching

Match different terms describing equivalent diseases or healthcare services using semantic similarity.

NLP-Specific Skills

01

Named Entity Recognition (NER)

Identifying important entities such as disease names and medical services.

02

Information Extraction

Extracting healthcare services and treatment capabilities from text.

03

Semantic Similarity

Finding similarities between different expressions with equivalent meanings.

04

Text Classification

Classifying textual information according to relevant categories.
