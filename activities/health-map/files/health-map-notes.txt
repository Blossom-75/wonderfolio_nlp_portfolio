HEALTH MAP
Idea:
A location-based, disease-aware healthcare recommendation system that identifies and ranks nearby healthcare facilities based on the user's location, disease-specific treatment capabilities, and travel accessibility.

Research Gaps:
1. Disease-specific facility matching Identifies healthcare centres that provide services relevant to the particular disease.
2. Treatment availability: Considers verified diagnostic and treatment capabilities of the healthcare center 
3. Travel accessibility: Ranks suitable facilities based on road distance and estimated travel time.

NLP Usage:
1. Extract healthcare services and treatment capabilities from facility descriptions and other textual sources.
2. Identify disease names, medical services, and user search intent from natural-language queries.
3. Match different terms describing equivalent diseases or healthcare services using semantic similarity.

NLP-Specific Skills:

1. Named Entity Recognition (NER)
2. Information Extraction
3. Semantic Similarity
4. Text Classification
